## 🚗 Overview

**True Wheel Motors** is an executive Irish automotive import portal specializing in hand-picked Grade 4.5+ inspected vehicles sourced directly from premier Japanese auction halls (USS Tokyo, HAA Kobe, JU Auctions).

We handle the entire journey:
- Direct Japan auction access and translation of official inspector sheets.
- 120-point mechanical and BIMTA genuine odometer verification.
- Insured Roll-on/Roll-off marine transit directly to Dublin Port.
- Irish Revenue AIS/CDS customs declarations, EU-Japan EPA 0% Customs Duty relief, and VRT clearance.
- 2-Year NCT guarantee, factory immobilizer certification, Apple CarPlay touchscreens, and nationwide Irish delivery.
