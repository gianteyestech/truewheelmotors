Statutory Formula

How VRT Is Calculated

Vehicle Registration Tax is a statutory charge applied the first time an imported vehicle is registered in the Republic of Ireland. It is based on Irish Revenue's Open Market Selling Price (OMSP)—not what you paid at auction in Japan.

VRT = (CO₂ Band Rate × Revenue OMSP) + NOx Environmental Levy − BEV Relief

Lower WLTP emissions place your vehicle into a lower percentage band. The NOx levy is added progressively for combustion engines, and qualifying electric vehicles receive up to €5,000 relief.

The Smart Irish Motorist Choice

Why Import From Japan to Ireland?

Ireland and Japan are both Right-Hand Drive (RHD) countries, making Japanese imports a seamless match for Irish roads with unmatched build quality and value.

Right-Hand Drive Match

Unlike imports from the EU continent or USA, Japanese vehicles are manufactured native Right-Hand Drive (RHD). Steering wheel, headlights beam-pattern, and controls are 100% compliant with Irish roads.

Rust-Free Chassis

Japanese roads in metropolitan regions (Tokyo, Osaka, Yokohama) do not use corrosive rock salt during winter. Undercarriages, suspension arms, and brake lines remain in factory-fresh rust-free condition.

World-Leading Hybrids

Japan is the pioneer of hybrid powertrain engineering. Models like the Toyota Aqua, Prius, Lexus F-Sport, and Honda Vezel deliver 65-75+ MPG with exceptionally low Irish road tax (€170-€190/yr).

Strict 'Shaken' Inspection

Japan's bi-annual vehicle inspection test ('Shaken') is amongst the world's most rigorous. Japanese drivers service their cars obsessively, resulting in pristine mechanical health and low genuine mileage.
